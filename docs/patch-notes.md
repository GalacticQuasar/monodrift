## 2026-11-21

- Covers: `83dd017` to `208fb9d` (multiplayer rooms, curb, grip, start screen hints)
- Last commit: `208fb9d`

**Monodrift**: a minimal top-down drift game. Play at https://monodrift.galacticquasar.dev
What's new: https://monodrift.galacticquasar.dev/updates

**Big new stuff**
- **Drive with a friend**: tap "Drive with a friend" on the start screen (or Friends in Settings) and send your friend the link. You both drive the same road at the same time and see each other's car, with an arrow at the screen edge when they're out of view
- **Rounds**: when you're both ready, a 3-2-1 countdown starts the round. Crash first and you watch your friend until they crash too, then see who went further and go again. Pick a random road or today's Daily road. Room runs count for the leaderboard like normal runs

- **Curb**: a grey curb now runs along both edges of the road. You can drive on it; the run ends past it
- **Grip**: the car grips a little less, so slides last slightly longer

**Polish**
- **Start screen**: on a computer it shows the keys to steer with, and on a phone in Sides mode, arrows at the screen edges show where to hold

## 2026-11-13

- Covers: everything from `a06b2f5` (migration to Vite + TypeScript) on
- Last commit: `8862b09`

**Monodrift**: a minimal top-down drift game. Play at https://monodrift.galacticquasar.dev

**Big new stuff**
- **Leaderboard**: pick a name after a run, and your best scores go on the board with Today and All-time rankings
- **Daily road**: switch Road to Daily and everyone drives the same road that day. A new one comes out at midnight UTC
- **Install it as an app**: add it to your phone's home screen and it opens full screen
- **Works offline**: after your first visit you can play with no signal, even in airplane mode
- **Pixel-art trees** along the road
- **Zen mode**: no crashes and no score, just drive

**Controls and feel**
- Laptop: arrow keys or A/D to steer, any key to start or retry
- Phone: hold the left or right side, or use Slide mode to drag and aim
- No more buzzing when you hold your finger on the screen
- The road on the start screen is the one you drive
- A short pause after crashing so you don't skip past your score
- The mouse cursor hides while you play
- Smoother frame rate

**Polish**
- New name and web address, a tab icon, and a preview card when you share the link
